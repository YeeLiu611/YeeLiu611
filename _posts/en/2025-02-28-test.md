\# Goal One: To link Person-Centered communities in the Asian region and around the world.

Projects: Asia Society, Asian Person-Centred Congress, Person-Centered Asia Journal, Roundtable Forums

We have currently established links with representatives from Japan and Vietnam. In the future, we will continue to connect with communities in more Asian countries and regions. We expect to run an Asian Person-Centered Congress within a year, offering workshops, congregational heart groups and forums in different languages, so that Person-Centered practitioners from different countries will have the opportunity to interact in a local Asian conference. We plan to hold one or more round-table forums where Person-Centered practitioners from different countries will be invited to exchange ideas on a pressing issue of the day. We plan to establish an Asian-based academic journal on Person-Centeredness within 3 years.

In addition, we have contacted the Person-Centered World Association (PCE-World). PCE-World will form a working group to support us in creating an Asian Association for Person-Centered Counselling and Therapy. The Asian Association will take on the functions of accreditation of training programmes, registration and recognition of counsellors, and practice support.

How you can get involved: In addition to signing up for conferences, forums, and submitting articles to journals in the future, we welcome you to join as a volunteer on our board. \# Goal 2: Supporting the ideas and creativity of individuals and organisations

Projects: Human Centred Incubator Project, Joint Project, Internal Foundation

We will establish an internally managed foundation at The Person-Centered Institute of Asia (PCIA), which will receive a percentage of funds from all of PCIA's for-profit projects, as well as donations. The foundation will be open to public scrutiny and its financial reports will be made available to the public on a regular basis.

The Foundation will provide funding for Human Incubator projects. Any organisation or individual with an idea that is in line with Person-Centered values (whether it be caring for left-behind children, counselling or feminist workshops, or a research project) can apply for support from PCIA, which will provide support in terms of staffing, funding, project supervision, and international connections, depending on the actual funding and resources available. The project does not need to be very ambitious, it could even be an independent consultant who wants to provide free counselling to a vulnerable group of people for a certain period of time, but needs additional funding to support the loss of income as a result.

Incubator projects can also be for-profit in nature (e.g. a counsellor who wants to do fee-based drop-in groups or workshops). We will provide the same level of support for for-profit projects, while receiving a portion of the project profits to support the day-to-day operations of PCIA, as well as into the Foundation to support other non-profit projects.

How you can get involved: You can apply for a project as an individual or an organisation, make a donation to our Foundation, or volunteer for one of our incubator projects.

\# Goal 3: Support the Learning and Growth of Person-Centered Counselors

Projects: Asia Society Person-Centered Counselor Registration System and Program Certification System, 2-year Systematic Training on Person-Centered Counseling, Basic Introduction to Person-Centered Counseling, Person-Centered Group Supervision, Meeting Heart Groups, etc.

We will establish a counsellor registration system and program certification system in the Asian Person-Centered Association to provide some kind of ‘endorsement’ and organisational guarantee for the practice and development of Person-Centered Counsellors. In addition, we will begin to plan for the establishment of a 2-year long training programme that is fully aligned with Person-Centered principles. More recent projects are basic introductory courses in Person-Centered counselling, centering groups, group supervision, etc. All of the for-profit programmes in this section provide a percentage of funds to our Foundation to support our non-profit programmes.

How you can get involved: Attend our courses and trainings, join as a member of the Asian Person-Centered Association in the future, learn about the registration and certification system that will be coming online in the future, and communicate with us and provide input.

\# Goal 4: Work outside the professional field of counselling such as education, interpersonal, conflict regulation, etc.

Projects: Listening Space, Education Project, Mediator Project, and more to come!

We will prepare listening space projects, i.e. for lay people as well as for counselling professionals, so that more people can experience the power of listening. We will launch new educational projects and promote free learning within a certain scope, in association with schools or educational communities. We will launch a mediator project, building on the strengths and traditions of the Person-Centered approach to conflict regulation, by training mediators to support the various types of conflicts that occur in the community.

How you can get involved: participate in our projects, join as a volunteer, provide new project ideas, or give us feedback and ideas.
