# **About the Person-Centered Institute of Asia**


The full name of The Person-Centered Institute of Asia (PCIA) is The Person-Centered Institute of Asia. Our legal entity is registered in Hong Kong. It is a Person-Centered institute for all of Asia. The WeChat subscription account of PCIA is currently hosted by Karl Rogers (Shenzhen) Consulting Services Co., Ltd.


# **We want to make the world a little better**


Last autumn, Annzo and I went to Chicago to visit our Person-Centered teachers. One afternoon we sat down to chat with Margret Warner. We asked her, ‘Why is there such a high proportion of homosexuals in the Person-Centered community?’ She said, ‘**Because in the 1950s, Carl Rogers’ counselling centre was the only place in North America that did not consider homosexuality a disease**.’ When she said this, something struck my heart, and I suddenly sank into an indescribable emptiness and a deep feeling of acceptance. At that moment, I seemed to see **those who had been excluded, alienated and labelled as ‘sick’ in mainstream society more than half a century ago finally having a space where they did not have to pretend and were not humiliated.** This was not the first time I had been deeply moved in this way. A few years ago, at the ADPCA conference, Gay Leah Barfield, who was in her 90s and was the head of Rogers' “world peace project” at the time, told us a story: ’Many, many years ago, a former Nazi officer and a concentration camp survivor came to one of our meetings. At first, they bore an unforgiving hatred for each other. But after a week of meetings, they came to love each other deeply. ‘There was silence in the room.**What kind of power could make hatred dissolve, pain heal, and two people who should be enemies fall in love?**


Over the years, I have been thinking about what is at the heart of a Person-Centered approach. There seem to be so many different answers to this question, but what remains unchanged in all of them is a deep respect and trust in people, an attitude that treats people as people. Treating people in a person-centered way and treating life in a life-centered way is not always easy. Growing up in contemporary China, I don't really understand what it feels like to be allowed to be yourself.**Instead of having the right to define oneself, one is defined by exam scores, marriage and childbirth, behaviour norms, wealth and status.**Despite decades of economic take-off, the phenomenon of ‘not treating people as people’ has intensified; in the crowd chasing the wind, there are always people who are ruthlessly left behind.


I remember when I was in primary school, my English teacher publicly commented on me because I got a poor exam result: ‘Your parents are a disgrace for giving birth to such a stupid child.’ I often imagine that if I were a primary school teacher today and met a child with poor grades, I might pat him and tell him, ‘You didn't do well, and I know you're already very sad. It's really not easy. **I just want you to know that I will be here with you. You are important.**‘When our society treats people as a means to an end rather than an end in itself, people cannot be themselves, and the relationships and attitudes between people are therefore full of cruelty rather than softness and warmth.


We live in a land full of deep historical trauma. The English teacher who humiliated me was not born with malicious intent. It's just that our history and culture have not given her a gentle soil in which she can deeply become herself. As I met one after another visitor in the counselling room who could not become themselves due to the barren environment, I gradually understood why Rogers shifted his interest from counselling to a larger field in the second half of his life. Because if we do not change the world we live in, we can only do passive remedies forever. And if we can influence everyone around us a little bit, so that they can have a little more space to exist as themselves, their attitude towards others will change a little bit. **When a community has the space for everyone to be themselves, the collective will naturally develop the resilience to embrace differences and the warmth to value humanity.**


This is the vision of The Person-Centered Institute of Asia. The Person-Centered approach has never been just a model or technique for counselling. It is more like a way of being, a fundamental view of life and humanity. **It recognises that every life has its own irreplaceable value, no matter what form it takes.** We treat a person in good health in the same way as someone who is in a difficult situation; we see someone who is similar to us in terms of their background in the same way as someone who is completely different. There is so much light and possibility in human nature, and we want to gently bring it to light. I often think of Rogers' efforts in the 1960s on issues of race, conflict mediation and educational liberation. Today, we hope to recapture this spirit in the Asian region – not just in counselling rooms, but also taking root in society and culture more broadly. **And everyone who reads this article has the opportunity to get involved in this cause.**


Next, I will go into detail about the projects and operating methods of The Person-Centered Institute of Asia and how everyone can get involved:


# **Goal 1: Linking Person-Centered Communities in the Asian Region and Around the World**


**Projects:** Asia Society, Person-Centered Asia Conference, Person-Centered Asia Journal, Roundtable Forums


**Introduction:**


We have already established contact with representatives from Japan and Vietnam. In the future, we will continue to establish contact with communities in more Asian countries and regions. We plan to hold an Asian Humanistic Conference once a year, offering workshops in different languages, mindfulness groups and forums, so that Person-Centered practitioners from different countries have the opportunity to exchange at a conference based in Asia. We plan to hold one or more roundtable forums, inviting Person-Centered practitioners from different countries to exchange on a pressing issue of the day. We plan to establish an Asian-based Person-Centered academic journal within three years.


In addition, we have already contacted the Person-Centered World Association (PCE-World). The World Association will set up a working group to support us in establishing an Asian association for Person-Centered counselling and therapy. The Asian association will take on functions such as training programme accreditation, counsellor registration and recognition, and practice support.


**How you can get involved:** In addition to registering for conferences, forums and contributing to the journal in the future, we welcome you to join us as a volunteer in this area. As a volunteer, you will gain experience, support and additional resources as you work with a team that is committed to the Person-Centered approach and witness the establishment of these projects. We are happy to provide volunteers with the required proof of volunteering and references.


# **Goal 2: Support the ideas and initiatives of individuals and organisations**


**Projects:** Person-Centered Incubator Project, Joint Projects, Internal Foundation


**Description:**


We will establish an internally managed foundation at The Person-Centered Institute of Asia (PCIA). PCIA will allocate a percentage of the profits from all its for-profit projects to this foundation, and will also accept donations. The foundation will be open and transparent, and its financial reports will be made public on a regular basis.


The foundation will provide funding for the Person-Centered Incubator. Any organisation or individual with an idea that aligns with Person-Centered values (whether it is care for left-behind children, counselling or feminist workshops, or a piece of research) can apply for support from the PCIA. The PCIA will provide support in the form of, but not limited to, personnel support, financial support, project supervision, and international connections, depending on the actual funds and resources. The project does not need to be very ambitious. It can even be the case that an independent consultant wants to provide free consultation to a certain disadvantaged group for a period of time, but needs additional funding to support the loss of income.


Incubator projects can also be for-profit in nature (for example, a consultant wants to charge for a group or workshop). We will provide the same support for for-profit projects, while charging a portion of the profits of the project to support the daily operations of PCIA and enter the foundation to support other non-profit projects.


**How you can get involved:** You can apply for projects as an individual or an organisation, make donations to our foundation, or become a volunteer in our incubator project.


# **Goal 3: Support the learning and growth of Person-Centered consultants**


**Projects:** Asia Association Person-Centered Consultant Registration System and Project Certification System, 2-year systematic training in Person-Centered Consulting, Person-Centered Consulting Basic Introductory Course, Person-Centered Group Supervision, Empathic Groups, etc.


**Introduction:**


We will establish a consultant registration system and project certification system at the Asia Person-Centered Association to provide a certain ‘endorsement’ and organisational guarantee for the practice and development of Person-Centered consultants. In addition, we will begin planning the establishment of a two-year long-term training that fully complies with Person-Centered principles. More immediate projects include introductory courses on the fundamentals of Person-Centered counselling, appreciative groups, group supervision, etc. A certain percentage of the profits from for-profit projects in this sector will be provided to our foundation to support our non-profit projects.


**How you can get involved:** by attending our courses and training, by joining as a member of the Person-Centered Association of Asia in the future, by getting to know the registration and certification system that will be launched in the future, and by communicating with us and providing your feedback.


# **Goal 4: Work outside the professional field of counselling, such as education, interpersonal relationships, conflict resolution, etc.**


**Projects:** Listening Lab, education projects, mediator projects, and more projects to come in the future


**Introduction:**


We will prepare a listening laboratory project, both for non-professionals and for psychological counselling professionals, so that more people can experience the power of listening. We will launch new educational projects and, to a certain extent, promote the development of free learning through collaboration with schools or educational communities. We will launch a mediator project to leverage the advantages and traditions of Person-Centered approaches to conflict resolution and support various conflicts that occur in the community through the training of mediators.


**How you can get involved:** Join our projects, become a volunteer, contribute new project ideas, or give us feedback and suggestions.
